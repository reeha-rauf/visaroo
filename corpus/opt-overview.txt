Optional Practical Training (OPT) for F-1 Students

Optional Practical Training (OPT) is temporary employment that is directly related to an
F-1 student's major area of study. Eligible students can apply to receive up to 12 months
of OPT employment authorization before completing their academic studies (pre-completion)
and/or after completing their academic studies (post-completion).

To apply for OPT, a student requests a recommendation from their Designated School
Official (DSO), who makes the recommendation in SEVIS. The student then files Form I-765,
Application for Employment Authorization, with USCIS. A student may not begin working until
they receive their Employment Authorization Document (EAD).

Post-completion OPT: Students may apply up to 90 days before completing their degree and no
later than 60 days after their program end date. The application must be filed within 30
days of the DSO entering the OPT recommendation in SEVIS.

Unemployment while on post-completion OPT: Students may not accrue an aggregate of more
than 90 days of unemployment during the initial 12-month OPT period.

This document reflects general USCIS guidance and is informational only.
