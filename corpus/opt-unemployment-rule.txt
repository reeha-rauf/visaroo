OPT and STEM OPT Unemployment Limits (8 CFR 214.2(f)(10) and (f)(12))

Federal regulations limit how long an F-1 student may be unemployed while on Optional
Practical Training.

Standard post-completion OPT: An aggregate of no more than 90 days of unemployment is
permitted during the initial period of post-completion OPT.

STEM OPT extension: A student granted the 24-month STEM OPT extension may be unemployed for
an aggregate of no more than 150 days over the total OPT period (the initial 90 days plus
an additional 60 days during the STEM extension).

Counting unemployment: Each day during the authorized OPT period that the student does not
have qualifying employment counts toward the limit. Time spent outside the United States
may or may not count depending on circumstances. Students should track their unemployment
days carefully and keep records of employment.

Exceeding the limit: A student who exceeds the unemployment limit is generally considered to
have failed to maintain status.

This document summarizes 8 CFR 214.2(f) and related USCIS guidance and is informational only.
