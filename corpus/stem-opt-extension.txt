STEM OPT Extension (24-Month Extension of Optional Practical Training)

F-1 students who have earned a degree in a qualifying science, technology, engineering, or
mathematics (STEM) field and who are participating in post-completion OPT may apply for a
24-month extension of their OPT employment authorization.

Eligibility includes working for an employer enrolled in E-Verify and completing Form
I-983, "Training Plan for STEM OPT Students," which outlines the training the student will
receive.

Filing window: A student should file Form I-765 for the STEM OPT extension up to 90 days
before their current OPT employment authorization expires. If the student files on time,
employment authorization is automatically extended for up to 180 days while the extension
application is pending.

Unemployment: During the combined OPT and STEM OPT period, a student may not accrue an
aggregate of more than 150 days of unemployment.

Reporting: STEM OPT students have additional reporting requirements, including validation
reports every six months and reporting material changes to the I-983 training plan.

This document reflects general USCIS and SEVP guidance and is informational only.
