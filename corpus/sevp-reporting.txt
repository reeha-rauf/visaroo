SEVP Reporting Requirements for F-1 Students on OPT and STEM OPT

F-1 students participating in OPT and STEM OPT must keep their SEVIS record current by
reporting certain changes to their Designated School Official (DSO) within 10 days of the
change.

Reportable events include:
- A change of legal name or residential or mailing address
- A change of employer (name and address of the new employer)
- A change in employment status, including loss of employment or a period of unemployment
- An interruption of employment

STEM OPT students must additionally report any material change to the Form I-983 training
plan and must submit a validation report to their DSO every six months confirming the
accuracy of their SEVIS information.

Failure to report required changes within the timeframe can be considered a failure to
maintain status.

This document reflects general SEVP (Study in the States) guidance and is informational only.
