F-1 Grace Periods and Maintaining Status

F-1 students are admitted for "duration of status" (D/S), meaning they may remain in the
United States as long as they maintain their status.

60-day grace period: After successfully completing a program of study and any authorized
period of post-completion OPT (including a STEM OPT extension), an F-1 student generally
has a 60-day grace period to prepare to depart the United States, transfer to another
SEVP-certified school, change education level, or take action to change to another status.

15-day grace period: An F-1 student authorized to withdraw from classes with DSO approval
generally receives a 15-day grace period. A student who fails to maintain status is
generally not eligible for a grace period.

Travel and re-entry: To re-enter the United States in F-1 status, a student generally needs
a valid passport, a valid F-1 visa stamp, and a current Form I-20 with a valid travel
endorsement from the DSO. Students on OPT or STEM OPT should also carry their EAD and
evidence of employment.

This document reflects general USCIS and SEVP guidance and is informational only.
